Child safety standards

Last updated 24 October 2026 · Operated by ASI-DOCTOR, Iraq · Contact asidoctoriq@gmail.com

Asi is an app for booking appointments with doctors. ASI-DOCTOR has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any content or behaviour of that kind is forbidden in Asi, and we act on it as described below.

Where people can communicate in Asi

  • Messages between a patient and the doctor they have booked with. Patients cannot message other patients.
  • Reviews that patients write about a doctor, shown publicly on that doctor's profile.

Every doctor account is checked and approved by an Asi administrator before it can receive bookings or messages.

What is prohibited

  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material.
  • Grooming, sexualising or exploiting a child in any way.
  • Using Asi to contact a child for any sexual purpose.

How to report a concern

Email asidoctoriq@gmail.com with the subject Child safety. Include the name of the account, and where you saw the content (a message or a review) if you can. You do not need an Asi account to report.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police first.

What we do with a report

  • We review every child safety report as a priority.
  • We remove violating content, such as a review, and close the account responsible.
  • We report confirmed child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the relevant national and regional authorities, and cooperate with law enforcement requests.

Child safety contact

ASI-DOCTOR's designated contact for child safety, and for questions about these standards, is asidoctoriq@gmail.com.
