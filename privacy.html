CliniBook

Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026 · Operated by ASI-DOCTOR, Iraq · Contact asidoctoriq@gmail.com

CliniBook lets patients find a doctor, book an appointment and message the clinic. Using it means we hold some information about you, including information about your health. This page says exactly what, and why.

What we collect

You give us

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number and a password. Doctors also give a specialty, licence number, clinic address and consultation fee.
  • Optional profile details — date of birth and gender.
  • Messages you send to a doctor through the app.
  • Reviews you write, which are shown publicly next to the doctor along with your name.

Created by using the app

  • Appointments — which doctor, when, the status, and the fee at the time of booking.
  • Consultation notes written by your doctor about a visit. This is health information and we treat it as the most sensitive thing we hold.
  • A device registration so we can send you a notification when an appointment changes or a message arrives.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or anything about what you do in other apps. There is no advertising in CliniBook and we do not sell data to anyone.

Why we hold it, and who sees it

  • Your name and specialty-level details are shown to the doctor you book with, so they know who is coming.
  • Your consultation notes are visible to you and to the doctor who wrote them. Not to other doctors, and not to other patients.
  • Administrators of the clinic can see accounts and bookings in order to run the service and approve doctors.
  • Reviews are public, by design. Do not put anything private in one.

We share data with no one else, except the two services the app needs to work: Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers the notification to your phone, and our own hosting provider, which runs the server. Firebase receives the notification text and a device token — not your medical record.

How it is protected

  • Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash. Nobody here can read them.
  • Access is controlled by role: a patient cannot read another patient's record, and a doctor cannot read a record from a clinic they are not part of.
  • Connection security: the app currently reaches our server over plain HTTP. Data travelling between your phone and the server is not encrypted, and on an untrusted network it could in principle be read by someone else. We are moving the service to HTTPS; until then, please avoid using CliniBook on public Wi-Fi.

Your choices

  • See and correct your details in the app, under Profile.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone's settings; the app keeps working.
  • Close your account at any time — how to delete your account, which also sets out what is kept afterwards and for how long.
  • Ask us anything about your data at asidoctoriq@gmail.com.

Children

CliniBook is for adults. An account for a child should be held and managed by a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
