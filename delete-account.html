Delete your account

Applies to the Asi app (com.asidoctor.app). You do not need the app installed to make this request.

If you still have the app

Open Profile → Delete my account, confirm with your password, and the account closes straight away.

If you no longer have the app

Email asidoctoriq@gmail.com from the address your account is registered under, with the subject Delete my account. We reply within 30 days.

We ask you to write from the registered address because it is how we confirm the request is really yours. If you cannot, tell us your full name and phone number and we will verify another way.

What happens

As soon as the account is closed:

  • You are signed out and can no longer sign in.
  • Any appointment you had booked that had not happened yet is cancelled, and the doctor is notified so the slot is freed.
  • Your devices stop receiving notifications.
  • Your profile is withdrawn — you no longer appear to doctors, and doctors no longer appear to patients.

What is kept, and for how long

Closing an account does not erase everything immediately. Appointments that already took place, and any consultation notes a doctor wrote, are part of a clinical record and are held for a short period afterwards.

  • Kept for 30 days after the account is closed: past appointments and the consultation notes attached to them. They are deleted once the 30 days are up.
  • Removed from the app: your profile, your contact details as shown to others, and your device registrations.
  • Not kept: your password, which is stored only as a one-way hash and is discarded with the account.

To ask for complete erasure of the retained records as well, say so in your email. We will carry it out except where keeping a record is required of us by law.

حذف حسابك

إذا كان التطبيق مثبتاً لديك

افتح الملف الشخصي ← حذف حسابي وأكّد بكلمة المرور، ويُغلق الحساب فوراً.

إذا لم يعد التطبيق لديك

راسلنا على asidoctoriq@gmail.com من البريد المسجّل في حسابك، والعنوان: حذف حسابي. نردّ خلال ٣٠ يوماً.

ماذا يحدث

  • يتم تسجيل خروجك ولا يمكنك الدخول مرة أخرى.
  • تُلغى مواعيدك القادمة ويُبلَّغ الطبيب بذلك.
  • تتوقف الإشعارات على أجهزتك.
  • يُسحب ملفك الشخصي فلا يظهر للآخرين.

ما الذي يُحتفظ به

تبقى المواعيد السابقة وملاحظات الطبيب لمدة ٣٠ يوماً بعد إغلاق الحساب ثم تُحذف. لطلب المحو الفوري، اذكر ذلك في رسالتك.
